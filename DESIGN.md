## Brand & Style

This design system defines an intelligent, campus-scale platform engineered for universities, students, and campus administrators. The visual identity sits at the intersection of collegiate reliability and modern enterprise SaaS: structured, precise, and immediately welcoming. 

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Trustworthy & Authoritative:** Instills immediate confidence through grounded navy foundations, structured geometry, and crisp borders, reassuring students and university staff during high-stress recovery situations.
- **Intelligent & Advanced:** Evokes precision through intentional royal blue accents, data-dense status modules, and clear AI visual indicators that demystify algorithmic matching.
- **Approachable & Calming:** Replaces panic with clarity using open whitespace, light cool surfaces, and human-friendly typography that guides users through reporting and verification.

### Design Movement: Modern Collegiate SaaS
The aesthetic rejects ephemeral trends in favor of refined utility. It blends clean corporate architecture with warm collegiate touches: systematic 8pt spatial pacing, low-contrast border boundaries, and deep navy-tinted ambient shadows that feel structural rather than decorative.

## Colors

The palette establishes an institutional yet dynamic environment using layered blues, cool neutrals, and functional alerts.

### Primary Hierarchy & Accents
- **Primary Accent (`#2764E8` - Royal Blue):** Used for focal interactive touchpoints, primary actions, algorithmic similarity highlights, active tabs, and focused outlines.
- **Deep Navy (`#172D55`):** The structural anchor. Used for high-emphasis navigation chrome, primary headings, key dashboard cards, and solid base elements.
- **Secondary Accent (`#EDF4FF` - Light Blue):** Serves as an interactive wash, active state fill, hover highlight, and high-readability badge backing.
- **Page Canvas (`#F8FAFF`):** A soft, cool background that eliminates harsh optical glare and establishes distinct separation from content containers.
- **Surface Pure (`#FFFFFF`):** High-surface containers, modal dialogs, and elevated cards.

### Text & Neutral Tokens
- **Text Primary (`#15233D`):** High-contrast deep navy delivering sharp readability across headers and body copy.
- **Text Muted (`#738098`):** Balanced mid-tone slate for supporting metadata, subheadings, and contextual hints.
- **Border Default (`#E2E8F0`):** Structural partition boundary for containers, inputs, and dividers.
- **Border Subtle (`#E9EEF6`):** Secondary nested partition line for interior card divisions and inactive table cells.

### Functional Alerts
- **Success (`#15956A`):** Matched items, confirmed verifications, and resolved claim flows.
- **Warning (`#E9A23B`):** Pending approvals, potential match thresholds, and expiration alerts.
- **Error (`#DC5555`):** Unverified claims, duplicate alerts, and critical input errors.

## Typography

Typography pairs geometric structure with high-density utility:

- **Headlines (Manrope):** Geometric, modern, and sturdy. Manrope delivers immediate collegiate authority and architectural stability in hero dashboards, modal headers, and analytical metrics.
- **Body & Controls (Inter):** Systemic, neutral, and readable. Inter renders administrative datasets, item descriptions, and status badges with pristine optical clarity down to 11px.

### Hierarchy & Responsive Adjustments
Display and large headline styles scale down on mobile breakpoints to preserve vertical flow without sacrificing semantic authority. Use `label-sm` with slight tracking for all-caps table column titles, system badges, and AI confidence telemetry.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored on an 8pt spatial grid with standard fluid boundaries.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid, `margin`: `2rem` (max-width container: 1360px), `gutter`: `1.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid, `margin`: `1.5rem`, `gutter`: `1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column grid, `margin`: `1rem`, `gutter`: `1rem`.

### Pacing Principles
- Component internals rely on `space-xs` (4px) to `space-md` (16px) for compact, data-rich item cards and intake forms.
- Section boundaries and card group separations leverage `space-lg` (24px) through `space-2xl` (48px) to clearly demarcate search parameters from catalog results.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through low-opacity ambient shadows tinted with Deep Navy (`#172D55`) rather than generic black, paired with structural outlines.

### Elevation Tiers
- **Flat (Level 0):** Input fields, inline table cells, and subtle sub-containers. Styled with a `1px solid #E2E8F0` or `#E9EEF6` boundary and zero shadow.
- **Resting Cards (Level 1):** The standard layer for lost item grid items, administrator cards, and telemetry cards. Employs `border: 1px solid #E2E8F0` coupled with:
  `box-shadow: 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(23, 45, 85, 0.04), 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(23, 45, 85, 0.06)`.
- **Floating Overlays & Hover (Level 2):** Applied when cards are hovered, and for dropdown menus, navigation bars, and search flyouts. Employs:
  `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(23, 45, 85, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(23, 45, 85, 0.04)`.
- **Modals & Drawers (Level 3):** Verification flows, image match inspection modals, and primary dialogs. Employs:
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 32px -8px rgba(23, 45, 85, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(23, 45, 85, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The design system uses standard Level 2 roundedness, balancing modern friendly software with collegiate professionalism.

### Radius Distribution
- **Cards & Primary Modules (`rounded-lg` / 16px):** Lost and found preview cards, admin analytics blocks, and primary modal wrappers use 16px corner radii to frame content cleanly.
- **Interactive Controls & Inputs (`rounded` / 8px):** Form inputs, buttons, filter dropdowns, and alert notices use 8px for tactile clarity.
- **Indicators, Chips & AI Badges (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Status badges, tag filters, match percentage chips, and avatar outlines adopt full pill radii to visually contrast against square structural grids.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#2764E8` with white text, font `label-lg`, `rounded` (8px), padding `10px 18px`. Hover: darken to `#1F53C5` with subtle elevation. Focus: 2px offset ring in `#2764E8`.
- **Secondary:** Filled `#EDF4FF` with `#2764E8` text, `border: 1px solid transparent`. Hover: `#DCE8FD`.
- **Outline / Administrative:** White surface, `#172D55` text, `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Hover: `#F8FAFF` background with `#15233D` border.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Style:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, `rounded` (8px), height 44px, text `#15233D`, placeholder `#738098`.
- **Active / Focus:** Border changes to `#2764E8` with a 3px outer glow ring of `rgba(39, 100, 232, 0.15)`.
- **Campus Quick Search:** 48px height, leading search icon in `#738098`, trailing keyboard shortcut chip (`⌘K`) in `#EDF4FF`.

### Cards (Lost & Found Item Display)
- **Structure:** 16px corner radius, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, Level 1 navy-tinted shadow.
- **Image Header:** Aspect ratio 16:10 with light background fill, top border radius 16px. Floated over the image is the status badge (top-left) and the AI Match chip (top-right).
- **Body Content:** Item title in `Manrope` (`title-md`), campus building location and timestamp in `Inter` (`body-sm`) with `#738098` icon prefix.

### Status Badges & AI Similarity Chips
- **AI Match Indicator:** Pill shape, background `#EDF4FF`, text `#2764E8`, border `1px solid rgba(39, 100, 232, 0.2)`. Displays spark icon + "96% AI Match" in `label-sm`.
- **Status - Unclaimed:** Background `rgba(233, 162, 59, 0.12)`, text `#E9A23B`, border `1px solid rgba(233, 162, 59, 0.25)`.
- **Status - Verified / Returned:** Background `rgba(21, 149, 106, 0.12)`, text `#15956A`, border `1px solid rgba(21, 149, 106, 0.25)`.

### Interactive Prototype Tabs
- **Segmented Controls:** Horizontal background container in `#EDF4FF` with 8px radius and 4px internal padding.
- **Active Tab:** Solid `#FFFFFF` fill with Level 1 micro-shadow, text `#172D55` (font `label-md`). Inactive tabs display text in `#738098` with hover transition to `#15233D`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- **Unchecked:** `18px × 18px`, `border: 1.5px solid #E2E8F0`, background `#FFFFFF`, radius 4px (checkbox) or circle (radio).
- **Checked:** Background `#2764E8`, border `#2764E8`, white checkmark or center pip.
